About this project

Purpose of the site

Movie trailers are a major part of how films are marketed, but it’s hard to tell which ones actually connect with audiences. Ember looks at YouTube engagement data (views, likes, comments, trailer length, genre, and studio) to identify trends in trailer popularity and to explore how online engagement relates to audience interest and, potentially, box office performance.

Ember focuses on trailers and film marketing.

Assumptions

  1. 01YouTube engagement (views, likes, comments) is a reasonable signal of audience interest in a trailer.
  2. 02Trailer length and genre are useful ways to group and compare trailers.
  3. 03Engagement numbers are a snapshot taken when the data was collected. Comparisons need to account for collection dates and time since publication.
  4. 04Box office results come from a separate source, so any link to engagement is exploratory. We report correlation, not cause.
